Why no collaboration graph here
The reference dashboard structure leads this theme with a force-directed collaboration graph. For Agroscope that graph would be near-useless: only 23 unique contributors exist org-wide, and 17 of 20 repositories have exactly one contributor — there's barely any cross-repo collaboration to visualise. We show a contributor table instead. Neo4j also has no institutional-affiliation graph for Agroscope (no RorOrg node, no AFFILIATED_WITH edges), so a "who works in which group" view isn't buildable from the graph as it stands — tracked in What's missing.
